\begin{multicols}{2}
\begin{keybox}\textbf{A. Any energy problem.} (1) Draw the boundary; closed, open, or isolated? (2) List what crosses: $Q$, $W$ (type), mass. (3) Pick the balance: \eqp{F:energy:closed}, \eqp{F:cal:ledger}, or \eqp{F:energy:sfee}. (4) Write state relations (ideal gas, tables, phase). (5) Solve symbolically, then substitute with units. (6) Check sign, range, limits, and units.\end{keybox}

\begin{keybox}\textbf{B. Entropy audit.} (1) $\Delta S$ of each body via a reversible path (\pg{F:entropy:idealgas}). (2) Reservoirs: $Q/T$. (3) Sum $=\Sgen\ge0$. (4) For machines, also check $\eta\le\eta_C$ (\pg{sec:feasibility}).\end{keybox}

\begin{keybox}\textbf{C. Heat-transfer network.} (1) Sketch nodes (temperatures) and paths. (2) Each path's resistance: \eqp{F:transfer:Rwall}--\eqref{F:transfer:Rcontact}, convection from a correlation (\tref{T:correlations}), radiation via $h_r$ (\eqp{F:rad:hr}). (3) Combine series/parallel. (4) $\dot Q=\Delta T/R$. (5) Back out intermediate temperatures; if $h$ or $h_r$ depended on a guessed surface $T$, iterate once.\end{keybox}

\begin{keybox}\textbf{D. Transient.} (1) $Bi<0.1$? (\eqp{F:transfer:biot}). (2) $C$ and $K$ (or $\tau$). (3) Input? Use \eqp{F:transfer:input}; none: \eqp{F:transfer:lumped}. (4) Sanity: physical limits (boiling, freezing), sensor lag.\end{keybox}
\end{multicols}
