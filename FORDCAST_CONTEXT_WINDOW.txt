1. ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION
================================================================================

FordCast is a bilingual (EN/ES) Central Texas low-water-crossing trip-audit prototype built for Austin-area (TX-37) commuters: it combines the crossings on an Austin-area trip (`2,052` mapped crossings within 120 m, grouped by physical crossing area) with official closure reports, active weather alerts, available creek evidence, historical flood context, and explicit unknowns; a separate, clearly experimental upstream-gauge replay study explores whether warnings may precede downstream reference-gauge category thresholds across five archived Austin floods.

The public-facing tagline is: "Check the crossings on your trip." / "Revisa los cruces de tu trayecto."
The pilot badge is: "CENTRAL TEXAS PILOT" / "PILOTO DEL CENTRO DE TEXAS"

It is NOT a promise that a road is safe, open, passable, dry, or predictable. It is NOT a validated road-flood forecast system. It is NOT evidence that anyone has already been protected, reached, or changed behavior in the field.

That sensor gap is the exact reason TX-37 drivers need FordCast's "unmonitored crossing" audit.

3. THE PROBLEM, USER, VALUE, AND PRODUCT POSITION
================================================================================

Central Texas ("Flash Flood Alley" along the Balcones Escarpment) has over 2,000 mapped low-water crossings. Familiar TX-37 routes cross creeks with sparse road-surface sensors. City closure maps (`ATXFloods` pin map), regional weather warnings (`NWS` polygons), state highway cameras (`TxDOT DriveTexas`), and ordinary turn-by-turn navigation (`Google Maps` / `Waze`) each provide part of the picture in isolation.

FordCast's product contribution is a **trip-specific evidence view**:
  - Which low-water crossings sit within 120 m of a selected route corridor (with multi-arm municipal hardware records grouped into physical crossing areas).
  - What official (`ATXFloods` closures, `NWS` Flash Flood Warning/Emergency polygons) and creek-sensor (`USGS` OGC API v1 stage and 30-min rise rate) evidence is currently present.
  - What historical flood context exists nearby (`727` geocoded NOAA Storm Events reports within 2 km, `1996-2026`, deduplicated by authoritative `event_id`).
  - What remains **unknown** (crossings with no linked creek sensor, stale readings `> 80 min`, offline feeds, or routes leaving the 80 km coverage radius).

Primary user:
  - A TX-37 / Austin-area commuter, parent, or teen driver checking a familiar route before leaving during rain or flood watches/warnings.

Positioning relative to existing tools:
  - FordCast is a **supplementary trip-audit layer**, not a replacement for official closure systems (`ATXFloods`, `TxDOT DriveTexas`), emergency alerts (`NWS`), or road barricades.
  - It hands off up to 3 detour waypoints to Google Maps for live turn-by-turn navigation (only when in `Live` mode with no synthetic test hazard active).

4. SCIENTIFIC AND SAFETY BOUNDARIES (SEVEN HARD INVARIANTS)
================================================================================

1. Never use reassurance words for road status:
   - Banned words: `safe`, `clear`, `passable`, `open`, `normal`, `all clear`, `seguro`, `transitable`, `abierto` (except explicit negation in `onboarding.pledge.item3`: `"Never treat 'no rise detected' as a guarantee a road is safe"` and stripping the proper noun `"City of Austin Open Data"` in `contracts.test.ts`).
   - Enforced across all of `en.json`, `es.json`, and replay storyboard strings by `mobile/src/__tests__/contracts.test.ts`.

2. Triple-redundant status encoding (Color + Geometric Shape/Glyph + Text Label, zero traffic-light green):
   - `no_rise`: Cyan `#2BD9D9` circle + check (`"No rise detected"` / `"Sin crecida detectada"`)
   - `watch`: Amber `#F5A623` triangle + `!` (`"Watch"` / `"Precaución"`)
   - `avoid`: Coral-red `#FF5470` octagon + `!` (`"Avoid"` / `"Evite"`)
   - `closed`: Coral-red `#FF5470` octagon + crossbar (`"Flooded – Closed"` / `"Inundado – Cerrado"`)
   - `unknown`: Slate `#7A879C` dashed circle + `?` (`"Unknown – data offline"` / `"Desconocido – sin datos"`, or `"Not monitored"` / `"Sin sensor"`)
   - Official closure reports (`ATXFloods`) always override sensor states (`displayState = 'closed'`).

3. Downstream gauge proxy != road-surface overtopping:
   - `Tier A = 0` (`road_forecast_verified = false`). No crossing has surveyed road-overtopping stage ground truth.
   - Historical ground truth in the replay study is `proxy_kind: downstream_gauge_flood_stage` (when a downstream reference gauge crosses its USGS/NWS flood stage).
   - Gauge height is measured relative to the USGS station datum zero, not water depth over the road deck. Negative values mean water is below the station datum zero, not an instrument error.

4. Strict causal no-lookahead in replays:
   - At simulated time `simT`, an observation `i` is visible only if `t_obs(i) <= simT` AND `t_avail(i) = t_obs(i) + 60 min <= simT` (and outside any modeled outage window until `outage.end`).
   - Replay grids (`buildReplayDataset`) use causal prior-observation lookup (`0 <= t_grid - t_obs <= 20 min`), never snapping future readings backward.

5. Range-only timing windows:
   - Never display a single-minute countdown. Only conservative range buckets (`[0, 15]`, `[15, 30]`, `[30, 45]`, `[45, 60]`, `[60, 90]`, `[90, 120]`, `[120, 180]`) are used.
   - When the lower bound is `0` (`[0, 15]`), the UI and spoken alert state that water may already be at the crossing (`state.now`).

6. Unknown and partial-failure resilience:
   - Stale (`> 80 min` live / `> 60 min` background cache), failed, loading, malformed, or unmonitored data never collapses into a calm state.
   - HTTP 200 responses with malformed JSON structure (ATXFloods missing `attributes` array; NWS missing `features` array) throw errors so source health records `ok: false` and the Guardian cache retains prior valid hazards for that source.
   - `activeCachedHazards(cache, now)` prunes expired NWS alerts (`expiresAt <= now`) and stale (`> 60 min`) sources before proximity alerting.

7. Strict isolation of simulation / test state from live navigation:
   - Switching from `drill` to `live` clears drill state; switching storms during a moving-car demo immediately terminates any mismatched run (`awaitingStorm` + `isStormMismatch`).
   - Dropping a synthetic closure (`simulateHazardOnRoute`) tags the hazard with `test-`, prefixes any route alert with `TEST · `, hides `Go with Google Maps` and `Share route summary`, provides an explicit `"End test and recheck route"` (`endRouteTest()`) button, and is synchronously removed from `hazardsRef` before `planRoute` computes any live route.

5. DATASET FOOTPRINT AND SCIENTIFIC EVALUATION NUMBERS
================================================================================

Live Trip-Audit Footprint:
  - `2,052` Austin-area crossing records (`141` seed study crossings in `mobile/assets/seed/crossings.json` + `1,911` additional official ATXFloods records in `mobile/assets/seed/official_crossings.json`).
  - `19` Central Texas USGS stream gauges (`mobile/assets/seed/gauges.json`).
  - `727` geocoded NOAA NCEI Storm Events Flood/Flash Flood reports across 8 Central Texas counties (`1996-01-01` to `2026-06-15`) within 2 km of study crossings (`mobile/assets/history/crossing_history.json`), with authoritative `event_id` deduplication across grouped crossings.
  - Corridor buffer: `120 m` for route audit (`60 m` for active route alert scope; `40 m` in Chrome extension).
  - Coverage gate: `80 km` radius around Austin (`30.2672, -97.7431`), requiring `coverageFraction >= 0.85` AND zero out-of-radius vertices/segment midpoints.

Experimental Gauge Study (`mobile/assets/results/results.json`):
  - `141` study crossings: `Tier A = 0` road-verified, `Tier B = 30` R&D corridor candidates (`geometry_kind: schematic_corridor_connector`, `review_status: unverified_road_and_topology`), `Tier C = 111` unmonitored.
  - `4` primary-calibrated crossing records (`is_primary: true` in `links.json`) across `2` physical research corridors:
    1. `tc-167` (**Henry Marx @ Barton Creek**) — upstream gauge `USGS-08155240` (Barton Creek at Lost Creek Blvd), downstream reference gauge `USGS-08155300` (Barton Creek at Loop 360).
    2. `atx-19`, `atx-20`, `atx-21` (**E. William Cannon Dr** on **Onion Creek** — `Slave Arm (W)`, `Master Standpipe`, `Slave Arm (E)`) — upstream gauge `USGS-08158860` (Slaughter Creek at FM 2304), downstream reference gauge `USGS-08159000` (Onion Creek at US Hwy 183).
  - `5` historical Austin flood windows (`Halloween 2013`, `Memorial Day 2015`, `Halloween 2015`, `October 2018`, `July 2025`) + `36-hour` calm control window (`May 2015`).
  - Pooled Leave-One-Storm-Out (LOSO) Cross-Validation across `12` proxy event cases (`assumed_delay_min: 60`):
    * **Upstream Candidate Rule (`AVOID`)**: `4 / 12` hits (`POD = 33.3%`), `8` misses, `1` false alarm (`FAR = 20.0%`), `CSI = 0.308`, median lead time `65 min` (IQR `47.5–76.2 min`) relative to downstream reference-gauge flood stage.
    * **WGS84 Nearest-Gauge Baseline (`AVOID`)**: `4 / 12` hits (`POD = 33.3%`), `8` misses, `0` false alarms (`FAR = 0.0%`), `CSI = 0.333`, median lead time `65 min`.
    * **Upstream Candidate Rule (`WATCH`)**: `6 / 12` hits (`POD = 50.0%`), `6` misses, `3` false alarms (`FAR = 33.3%`), `CSI = 0.400`, median lead time `90 min`.
  - Honest conclusion: On this small 12-case proxy sample, the upstream rule did not beat the simpler nearest-gauge baseline (`4/12` hits and `65 min` median lead on both; `1` vs. `0` false alarms). FordCast displays this baseline comparison directly in `StormsScreen.tsx` and `/lab`.
