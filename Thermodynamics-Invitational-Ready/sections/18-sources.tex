\section{Source register}\label{sec:sources}
% ===================================================================
\textbf{Access context (build of \builddate):} the build environment's network policy blocked the Science Olympiad site, NIST, MIT, OpenStax, BIPM, and general web hosts (HTTP 403). No rules, host, or device documents were attached. Package registries were reachable, so authoritative \emph{datasets and models} were used through versioned software and computed locally. A web-search tool returned result summaries (snippets) only; snippet-level items are labeled as such and are not treated as verified. Full provenance with exact functions and settings: \texttt{sources.md} and \texttt{data/provenance.json} in the source package.
