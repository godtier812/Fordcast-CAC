\begin{keybox}\textbf{Kelvin required} in: $PV=nRT$; any $T_2/T_1$ or $\ln T$; Carnot; $\sigma T^4$ and $h_r$; $\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$; $RT\ln K$; Clausius--Clapeyron; kinetic speeds. \textbf{Celsius allowed} only for differences: $mc\Delta T$, $hA\Delta T$, $kA\Delta T/L$, $T-T_\infty$ in cooling laws.\end{keybox}

\textbf{Unit hazards that cost points}
\begin{itemize}
\item kJ vs J (heats of formation in kJ/mol, $R$ in J/(mol\,K)).
\item g vs kg (specific heat per gram vs per kilogram; molar mass in kg/mol for speeds).
\item L vs \si{m^3} ($\SI{1}{L}=\SI{e-3}{m^3}$; $\SI{1}{L.kPa}=\SI{1}{J}$; $\SI{1}{L.atm}=\SI{101.325}{J}$).
\item kPa vs Pa vs atm vs bar (\tref{T:conversions}).
\item Flux (\si{W/m^2}) vs rate (W); per-area R-value vs total resistance (K/W).
\item Thermochemical vs IT calorie; food Calorie = kcal.
\item Specific (per kg) vs molar (per mol) vs total (per object) heat capacities.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Dimension checks:} $[R T]=\si{J/mol}$; $[PV]=\si{J}$; $[hA]=[kA/L]=\si{W/K}$; $[\sigma T^4]=\si{W/m^2}$; $[C/K]=\si{s}$; $[\Delta H/R]=\si{K}$ (Clausius--Clapeyron, van 't Hoff slopes). Logarithms and exponentials need dimensionless arguments.

\textbf{Prefix and symbol collisions:} $\sigma$ (Stefan--Boltzmann) vs $\sigma_{\text{th}}$ (stress); $k$ (Boltzmann) vs $k$ (conductivity); $n$ (moles) vs $n_N$ (number density) vs $n_p$ (polytropic index); $\alpha$ (expansion, diffusivity, absorptivity); $\nu$ (kinematic viscosity, stoichiometric coefficient, frequency). Read the context.
